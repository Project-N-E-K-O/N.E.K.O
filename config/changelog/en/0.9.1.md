- **Cat Girl Visits**: Take your cat girl to visit a mutual friend. The two cat girls chat with each other, and each side can hear the other cat girl's voice. A new mutual-friends panel lets you send invitations directly, and after a visit you can preview it before saving it as a diary entry. Turn on the "Visits" switch in the settings menu to use it.
- **Watch Videos Together**: Paste a Bilibili link or let her pick a popular short video, and she reacts as you watch. Auto-watch plays videos back to back, and slash commands such as "/watch together" open it directly.
- **New Mini-games**: Added "Drawing Guess" and "Air Basketball".
- **Theater**: A new Theater mode: pick a script and act it out together with your cat girl. Performances can be archived.
- **Voiceprint and Voice Wake-up**: Once you enroll your voiceprint, she filters out the voices of anyone who hasn't enrolled, so she hears only you and recognizes you more accurately in noisy places. While on standby, say "Yui" or just speak in your enrolled voice to start a voice conversation. Voiceprint enrollment is now recorded in three parts.
- **A More Playful Cat**: The new "Gravity Cat" gives windows gravity, bouncy collisions, and drag inertia. The cat can sense desktop windows and interact with them, and settings now have a separate cat section.
- Added Zhipu GLM voice cloning and importing existing cloned voices, and upgraded ElevenLabs voices to v3 / v4 Turbo, plus extensive stability fixes and performance optimizations.
