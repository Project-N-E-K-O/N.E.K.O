# Cookie Policy

This policy explains how the Project N.E.K.O. documentation site uses browser storage and optional analytics cookies.

## Saving your choice

The site stores your analytics choice in your browser so it does not ask again on every visit. This saved preference is used only to remember whether analytics is allowed or declined.

## Optional analytics cookies

If you allow analytics, Google Analytics may use first-party cookies to distinguish visits and prepare aggregate usage reports. These reports help the maintainers understand documentation usage and visits to the Steam page.

Analytics cookies are not loaded before consent and are not used for advertising. If you decline analytics, optional analytics remains disabled.

## Retention and removal

The saved preference and optional analytics data are retained only as needed for their stated purposes and according to the applicable service settings. You can remove stored site data at any time through your browser.

## Managing your choice

Use the persistent **Cookie settings** control at the bottom of any documentation page to change your choice. Clearing the site's stored data resets the saved choice, so the site will ask again on a later visit.
